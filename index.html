Questions traders ask

Is it free to start?

Yes. You can start recording your sales for free. If any paid features come later, we will tell you clearly first.

Do I need a smartphone app?

No. You only need WhatsApp. There is nothing new to download or learn.

Is my information safe?

Your records are kept private. We do not share them with lenders or anyone else unless you agree.

How does my association join?

Send us a message using the form on our contact page or on WhatsApp. We will meet your leaders, set things up and help your members get started.
